# U.S. Equity Context Template

Use this only when the user's question asks how U.S. equity evidence relates to BTC, crypto or a token. A crypto-linked company name by itself does not select this template: standalone company fundamentals, valuation or event research uses the Standard Report Format or another matching original template.

## Skeleton

```text
**TL;DR**

Sentence 1 — answer the crypto-relevant business question.
Sentence 2 — state whether the equity evidence confirms, weakens, or leaves that judgment mixed.
Sentence 3 — give the 1-2 decisive numbers or dated facts.

🚨 **Notable anomalies:**

- material disagreement, stale/missing fields, event risk, or (none significant in this run)

———

**Details**

**Crypto signal**

- crypto evidence and its timestamp/window

**U.S. equity context**

- index, sector, proxy-equity, company, or event evidence actually returned

**Combined judgment**

- what agrees, what conflicts, and the practical implication

💡 **Takeaway:** one concrete next step or condition that would change the judgment.

🕐 timestamp · status · confidence
```

## Minimum

- Keep crypto evidence and U.S. equity context in separate blocks before combining them.
- Do not describe equity correlation, beta, leadership, or causality unless the executed result reports it.
- Preserve each result's session/window and do not mix current-session observations with completed-session facts.
- If several services disagree or use non-overlapping windows, state that limitation in `🚨 **Notable anomalies:**`.
- Omit any section whose evidence was not returned; never fill it from memory.
